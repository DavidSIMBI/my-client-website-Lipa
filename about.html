About us

About LipaGo

LipaGo is a student-focused marketplace idea that brings buyers, sellers, and service providers together in one friendly space.

Our purpose

Making student life easier

Students often need affordable course materials, everyday essentials, or practical help. LipaGo is designed as a simple place to discover these from other students.

What matters to us

Community

We aim to support useful connections among students in the same community.

Affordability

Second-hand items and student services can help make campus life more affordable.

Simplicity

The LipaGo concept keeps finding and sharing useful opportunities straightforward.
